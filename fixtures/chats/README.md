# Chats from other AI tools

Made-up samples in the forms the editor reads (`crates/core/src/chats.rs`): the data exports of
ChatGPT and Claude.ai (`conversations.json`) and a transcript of Claude Code. The Claude Code
sample follows a real transcript's structure, and `cowriting-plugin-log.jsonl` is a month's AI log
in the form the CoWriting plugin writes (the same as `fixtures/logs`); the two exports are written from what is known of
their form and were not compared with a real export.
