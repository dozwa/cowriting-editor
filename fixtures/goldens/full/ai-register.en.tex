\section*{AI register (disclosure of AI use)}

For “Walking breaks \& 100\% focus\_time”, the generative AI model Claude (Anthropic) was used via Claude Code with the CoWriting plugin in an assisting role only: for hints, questions, explanations and literature support. The manuscript text was written by the author. The plugin refuses the AI's writes to manuscript files, records any change it nevertheless detects during an AI tool call (listed below), and the manuscript's version history (git) shows how the text came about. All AI interactions were logged automatically (ai-log/). Some briefing lines for the reviewer personas (party.md) were proposed by the model from discussions with the author and taken over only after the author confirmed them.
